-- WontVoteIn(n, r): node n will not vote in round r
-- Equivalent to TLA+'s: (∀ v, ¬VotedForIn(n,v,r)) ∧ maxBal[n] > r
-- In Ivy terms: n hasn't voted in r, and n has joined some round > r

  (∀ v, ¬ vote n r v) ∧

--   - voted for v in c, or
