Simp steps and expression sizes are the more reliable scaling evidence.

Each transition has two alternatives with one higher-order existential witness,
unless b/w indicate otherwise. An opaque predicate of type Nat -> Bool -> Prop
is applied to f 0, so no matrix predicate takes a function-valued argument.
The final matrix is checked for residual existentials, and the outer prefix
must contain exactly d*b*w witnesses. This measures normalization only, not
kernel proof checking or complete SMT translation.

At depth 32 the normalized formula has exactly the input's 2,301 tree nodes,
but its normalization proof contains 608,230 allocated Expr objects and
11,411,585 tree occurrences. Structurally distinct proof subterms number
28,686. Thus linear formula size coexists with roughly cubic simplifier work
and proof allocation in this series. The guard remains small. Splitting trace
hypotheses early can avoid this normalization cost; local timings exclude
splitting/reassembly/context proof costs and do not establish linear end-to-end
behavior. The simp context is reused in local mode and setup is excluded.

With maxSteps=100000, depth 22 passes (90,088 steps), while 23 fails. The main
timing series raises maxSteps to expose scaling; raising that limit is not a
proposed fix. Heartbeat and recursion limits are also raised in every case.
