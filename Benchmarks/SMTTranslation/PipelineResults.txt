The retained artifact leaves all three resource limits at their Veil defaults.

Global +singlePass is not a verified substitute: on the depth 8 probe it left
Ring.Init, Ring.Next, Ring.relationalTransitionSystem._proof_1 and dispatcher
constants in the target. Attempting actual SMT translation then failed with
'cannot translate fun a b => Classical.propDecidable (a = b), SMT-LIB does not
support lambdas'. It stops unfolding too early for this pipeline.

Grounded likely prefix-cost mechanism, Lean v4.32.0
Lean/Meta/Tactic/Simp/Main.lean:403-410: simpForall opens one binder,
instantiates the remaining body, simplifies it, then abstracts the result and
proof and builds forall congruence. Lines 709-714 re-simplify a changed result
unless singlePass is enabled; reopening binders introduces fresh variables,
which can prevent exact-expression cache hits. The timings establish the
benefit of earlier introduction; allocation/proof-building percentages were
not profiled, so this is a source-supported explanation, not a measured cost
breakdown.

Historical limit distinction: lean-smt eea63e5 (2026-02-12) multiplied only
embedding's simp maxSteps by 100. Current pinned Normalize.lean/Embedding.lean
use singlePass=true and the default cap, following upstream #216. Veil's
nextSimp and existsQuantifierSimp are separate invocations and can fail first.
